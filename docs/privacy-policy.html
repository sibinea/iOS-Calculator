Privacy Policy

Simple Calculator · Last updated: August 31, 2026

Simple Calculator ("the App") is published by Sibin. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it on Android and other platforms.

Summary

Simple Calculator is an offline calculator. It does not require an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not collect the numbers you enter for analytics or advertising.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information through the App.

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect payment information.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs in the App.
  • Calculations stay on your device and are not sent to our servers.

Information Stored on Your Device

Calculator input and results exist only in the App’s memory while you use it. When you close the App, that session data is not saved unless your device operating system keeps the App in memory in the background. We do not operate cloud backup for calculator history.

Third-Party Services

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information related to app downloads, device type, crashes, or payments according to Google’s Privacy Policy. That collection is handled by Google, not by us.

Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Because the App does not transmit personal data to our servers, there is no online account or server-side storage for us to secure. You should still protect access to your device with a screen lock or equivalent security feature.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer through the support email listed on the Google Play Store listing for Simple Calculator.
